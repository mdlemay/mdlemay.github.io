My research has generated or influenced hardware and software security architectures deployed on a large proportion of computer systems in use today.

I have contributed to shipping processor security technologies, including https://www.forbes.com/sites/tonybradley/2020/06/17/intel-cet-raises-the-bar-for-malware-defense/[Control-Flow Enforcement Technology (CET)] and https://www.pcworld.com/article/619047/intels-12th-gen-vpro-chips-now-actively-fight-ransomware-supply-chain-attacks.html[VT-Redirect Protection (VT-rp)].

I was Principal Investigator for https://web.archive.org/web/20240917132526/https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/newsroom/news/intel-and-ucsd-join-darpa-cyberdefense-program.html[Intel's project on Cryptographic Capability Computing (C3)] in the https://www.darpa.mil/news-events/2022-10-13[DARPA HARDEN program].

I https://groups.google.com/a/chromium.org/g/memory-safety-dev/c/PDi1cvAvUGc[designed] and https://groups.google.com/a/chromium.org/g/memory-safety-dev/c/1tYGORldm3w[co-developed] deterministic spatial safety support for MiraclePtr in the Chrome browser.

My LLVM-based research on shielding stack memory from corruption helped lead to a 2022 paper in a top-tier programming languages conference https://www.zdnet.com/article/this-new-firefox-browser-feature-could-stop-zero-day-bugs-in-their-tracks/[covered by ZDNet].

I also researched approaches for scalable isolation, for example using https://plas2022.github.io/files/pdf/SegueColorGuard.pdf[segmentation to accelerate WebAssembly] (upstreamed in https://github.com/WebAssembly/wabt/pull/2395[wasm2c]).
